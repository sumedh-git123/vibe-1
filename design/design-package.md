# BidMate design package (Tier 1)

Not deployed. The build's input. Copy ships verbatim.

## Premise
BidMate turns a bid set into a Division 26 electrical takeoff that an estimator reviews and approves line by line.
Voice: a precise, calm instrument. Plain words. No AI framing, no model names, no confidence percentages.
One call to action: **Book a demo**.

## Palette (from the footage's world)
- ink      #0E1A26  canvas, deep blueprint navy (never pure black)
- ink-2    #152636  raised panels
- line     #7FB3CF  fine cyan linework
- paper    #ECE5D6  primary text, warm drafting paper
- copper   #D9955A  accent, rare doses (traced circuits, CTA)
Status (always hue + icon + label): Ready to review (cyan, open circle), Missing information (amber #E0B24A, triangle), Estimator approved (green #7CC49A, check).

## Below the fold
1. Problem, "4:00 p.m. on bid day." Body: "One missed disconnect. A few circuits nobody counted. That is the margin, gone. You answer for every number you submit, even the ones someone else counted. And there is never time to recount the whole set."
2. How it works, three steps:
   - Upload the set. "Drop in the drawings and specs. Clean vectors or rough scans, BidMate reads them sheet by sheet."
   - Review with the evidence. "Every quantity links to the exact spot on the sheet where it was found. Click it and see it."
   - Approve and bid. "You approve line by line. Only approved quantities go into your total."
3. Interactive moment, "Try the review." A mini workspace: approve sample lines, watch the approved total move. Layer toggles hide what is drawn, never what is counted.
4. Status labels, "Every screen speaks the same three words." Shape plus word plus color, prints clean in grayscale.
5. Warnings, "When something is off, it tells you four things." What we found / Why it matters / What to check / Where it is. Example: scale missing on E-201.
6. Principles: "Hiding a layer never changes the count." "Missing information blocks completion. No override." "Unverified measurements are drawn dashed."
7. FAQ (4).
8. Final CTA form, "See it on your own drawings." Name, company, work email, bids per month. Button "Book a demo".
9. Footer.

## Signature element
Copper circuit line that draws itself down the page, linking each section like a home run.
